Portfolio Governance & Overvalued Heavyweight Exclusions

To generate true alpha, we rigorously eliminate thematic dilution, auto-ancillary contamination, and inflated multiples. Here is why we bypassed popular index names like Hitachi, Motherson, and Bharat Forge.

Rejected Heavyweight Grid Power

Hitachi Energy India

Demanding 72x EV/EBITDA Multiple

Forces basket investors to swallow legacy low-margin grid divisions at 91x P/E with zero margin of safety.

Capitalsense Pure-Play Alternative:

Transformers & Rectifiers (TARIL) [14%]

Pure transformer monopoly at 18x EV/EBITDA with a ₹6,630 Cr order book.

Rejected Heavyweight Aerospace / Defense

Motherson & Bharat Forge

Auto-Ancillary Cyclicality

Fundamentally auto-ancillary suppliers where high-margin aerospace components represent a minor fraction of overall group revenues.

Capitalsense Pure-Play Alternative:

Dynamatic Tech [11%] & Azad Eng [10%]

100% pure-play Tier-1 aerostructure & turbine airfoil suppliers for Airbus/Boeing/GE.

Rejected Proxy Solar & Electronics

Waaree Energies & Dixon Tech

Commoditized Assembly / High P/E

Waaree rated Underperform by Jefferies due to module margin compression; Dixon trades at 69x P/E with memory-cost risks.

Capitalsense Pure-Play Alternative:

Emmvee Photovoltaic [11%] & Kaynes [13%]

Net-debt-free TOPCon cell makers & operational OSAT semiconductor packaging plays.
